Notes and reading paths on AI safety, existential risk and environmental change.

Most people who come to these topics find two things hard. The ideas themselves are dense, and the fields rarely talk to each other. Polaris notes is my attempt to make both easier.


## Where to start {#where-to-start}

-   **Reading paths** are guided routes through the material, for a specific course or question. [Browse reading paths](https://polarisnotes.github.io/tags/path)
-   **Concept notes** explain one idea in plain language and link it to related ideas. [Browse concepts](https://polarisnotes.github.io/tags/concept)
-   **Resource notes** summarise a paper, article, video or podcast, with my take and questions to think about. [Browse resources](https://polarisnotes.github.io/tags/resource)

Every page has a graph showing how it connects to other notes. Follow the links that catch your interest. That is often where the best ideas come from.


## Guest writers {#guest-writers}

I am inviting people who work in these fields to write here. Some have years of research behind them. Others changed careers, started an organisation or launched a project. Their stories show that getting into this work is more possible than it looks from the outside.


## Get involved {#get-involved}

If you would like to write a piece, suggest a resource or point out a mistake, I would like to hear from you. The [About page](https://polarisnotes.github.io/about) has the details.
