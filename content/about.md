## Why Polaris notes {#why-polaris-notes}

Facilitating AI safety courses, I meet many people coming into existential risk for the first time. They arrive curious and motivated, then get stuck. The core ideas are hard to grasp at first, and it is even harder to see how AI safety, wider existential risk and environmental change connect.

Polaris notes is my answer to that. Each note explains one idea, one resource or one route through the resources, and links to the others. The aim is to help you understand the field, remember what you read and notice connections that a single course rarely shows.

It is also meant to grow beyond me. I want to host writing from experienced people in the field, and from people who changed careers, started organisations or built projects. Many of them started where you might be now. Over time, I hope this becomes a shared project, built by everyone who wants to make this field easier to enter.

## Why "Polaris" {#why-polaris}

Polaris is the North Star. Sailors and travellers have used it for centuries to find their way, and it sits almost directly overhead at the North Pole, close to where much of my earlier work was focused. These notes are meant to work the same way, as a fixed point to orient by when a field feels large and unfamiliar.
